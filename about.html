About Mortgage Calculator

Mortgage Calculator is a free, multi-region online tool designed to help you understand and plan your mortgage payments. Supporting 5 regions — China Mainland, Hong Kong, United States, Japan, and Korea — each with local loan types, regulations, and calculation methods. Whether you are a first-time homebuyer or refinancing, our calculator gives you clear, detailed projections.

What We Offer

5-Region Support

China, Hong Kong, US, Japan, Korea — each with local loan types, rates, and regulations.

Local Loan Types

China HPF/combined loans, HK HIBOR/Prime plans, US fixed/ARM, Japan Flat 35, Korea jeonse loans.

Region-Specific Checks

HK stress test, US PMI & property tax, Japan tax deduction, Korea LTV/DTI/DSR compliance.

Two Repayment Methods

Compare Equal Monthly Installments (EMI) and Equal Principal payment plans side by side.

Detailed Schedule

View month-by-month or year-by-year breakdowns of principal, interest, and remaining balance.

5 Languages

简体中文, 繁體中文, English, 日本語, 한국어 — switch anytime, all calculations update instantly.

How It Works

All calculations run directly in your browser using JavaScript. Your loan details (amount, rate, term, down payment) never leave your device — we do not store, transmit, or have access to any data you enter. This means you can use the calculator with complete privacy.
