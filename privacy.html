Welcome to Mortgage Calculator ("we," "our," or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring you understand how your information is handled when you use our website (the "Service").

Our mortgage calculator processes the loan parameters you enter (loan amount, interest rate, loan term, down payment) entirely within your browser. We do not store, transmit, or collect any of the calculation data you input. All calculations are performed client-side using JavaScript.

When you visit our website, certain information is automatically collected through standard internet technologies:

  • You can also opt out of third-party vendor cookies at AboutAds.info.

3.3 Managing Cookies

You can control and delete cookies through your browser settings. Disabling cookies may affect the functionality of certain features on our Service. Refer to your browser's help documentation for specific instructions.
